Transparency

Sources and credits.

We build on the work of outstanding educators, researchers and open-source communities. This page records every source we use, how we use it, and the licence that applies.

How we reference

1 · In the lesson

Where an explanation is inspired by a source, the lesson says so and links to it.

2 · Continue exploring

Every lesson ends with resources that explain the same ideas from different angles.

3 · This register

The author, link, how we use it and the licence, for every source.

What our labels mean

Linked
We point learners to the original. Nothing is copied.
Referenced
We cite specific chapters or papers as further reading.
Inspired by
The source shaped our teaching approach; our text, diagrams and code are original.
Adapted from
We modified the source's material, under its licence, with required notices. Currently: none.
Reproduced
We used the source's material unchanged, with permission or under its licence. Currently: none.

We never present another educator's work as our own. All lesson text, diagrams, interactive labs, examples and quizzes on these pages are original Code With Impact material. Example organisations, people, documents and studies are fictional.
