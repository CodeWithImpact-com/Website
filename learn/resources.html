Books

Paid books, available through O'Reilly, Springer and good bookshops. Free code notebooks accompany several of them.

  • BookIntermediate

    Hands-On Large Language Models ↗(opens in a new tab)

    Jay Alammar & Maarten Grootendorst · O'Reilly, 2024

    Our main reference for LLM fundamentals: tokens, embeddings, transformers, prompting and RAG. Highly visual.

  • BookIntermediate

    An Illustrated Guide to AI Agents ↗(opens in a new tab)

    Maarten Grootendorst & Jay Alammar · O'Reilly, 2026

    The same authors' visual guide to agents: memory, tools, planning, evaluation and multi-agent systems.

  • BookAdvanced

    AI Engineering ↗(opens in a new tab)

    Chip Huyen · O'Reilly, 2024

    Turning models into reliable products: evaluation, RAG, agents, cost and deployment.

  • BookAdvanced

    Agentic Design Patterns: A Hands-On Guide to Building Intelligent Systems ↗(opens in a new tab)

    Antonio Gullí · Springer, 2025

    A catalogue of 21 agentic design patterns, each with explanation and code examples.
